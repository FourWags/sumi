The bundled main_en.dict and corresponding en_wordlist.combined.gz were taken
from that same upstream revision; the latter is supplied in dictionaries/.
The dictionary header identifies English (US). No proprietary gesture libraries
or externally sourced prebuilt keyboard native libraries are included. All IME
native libraries are built from the included C++ source using the Android NDK.

Runtime dependencies are AndroidX Core, Collection, RecyclerView and their
transitive dependencies, plus Kotlin standard libraries. Their published license
notices are retained in dependency artifacts/APK metadata. AndroidX and Kotlin
are open-source platform libraries, not cloud or analytics SDKs. JSR-305 is a
compile-only annotation dependency and is not shipped as a runtime library.
JUnit and AndroidX Test are development-only test dependencies.

Sumi vector icons and the two bundled Bloom/Wave GIF animations were created
for this project. They are new interpretations of the supplied design direction,
not recovered production assets. Preview sheets are supplied beside this project.
